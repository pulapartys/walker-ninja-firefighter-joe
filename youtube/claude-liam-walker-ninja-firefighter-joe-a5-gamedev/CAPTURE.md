- **Hose take (B07):** Godot 4.7.2 Movie Maker, `capture/film_capture.gd` on an isolated copy of `godot/` (from `git archive e8c2a30`) with `capture/override.cfg` (window 3840×2160; the 640×360 game is shown through its own `canvas_items` stretch) and `--fixed-fps 30`. Scripted normal input through the player's test hooks via `tests/route_driver.gd`: Extinguisho walks and jumps from the spawn to the blocking fire, taps W in range, stands while the water runs, and the take ends 1.5 s after the water stops. No position or velocity edits. 16.8 s, MJPEG 3840×2160, the game's own audio track (not used in the film).
- `game.test_mode = true` is set by the capture driver; its only effect in `session.gd` is to disable the pause on window-focus loss, which Movie Maker triggers because the capture runs unfocused. The first take paused at tick 81 for exactly that reason and was discarded (`capture/logs/hose-godot.log` is the successful run).
- Per-tick logs in `capture/logs/`: `hose-inputs.jsonl` (every scripted press), `hose-states.jsonl` (state, pose, position, `hosing`, `water_ticks`, and the glow uniform). Hosing runs from t = 10.30 s to 15.27 s; glow peaks at 0.799 and is 0.000 at the end.
